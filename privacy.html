Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short:

  • Your CV and the job ad are processed only in your browser. They are never uploaded, stored or seen by us or anyone else.
  • We use Google Analytics only if you accept cookies, to count visits and how often the checker is used.
  • You can change your cookie choice at any time via Cookie settings at the bottom of the main page.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of this website (https://burcuxx.github.io/ats-checker/) is Burcu Ozcan, based in Malta. For any privacy question or request, contact burcukozcan@gmail.com.

2. Your CV and the job ad

When you upload a CV (PDF, DOCX or TXT), paste text or paste a job ad, all reading and analysis happens on your own device, inside your browser. The file and its contents are not sent to any server, not stored after you close or reload the page, and not shared with anyone. The site's security policy technically blocks it from sending data anywhere other than the services listed below.

To read PDF and Word files, your browser downloads two open-source libraries (pdf.js and mammoth.js) from cdnjs. These are code downloads only; your file is never sent to cdnjs.

3. Information stored in your browser

We save three settings in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them: your language, your theme (light/dark) and your cookie choice. They stay on your device, are never sent to us, and you can delete them by clearing your browser's site data.

4. Google Analytics (only with your consent)

If you click Accept in the cookie banner, we load Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Before you accept, no request is made to Google.

What is collected: pages viewed, approximate location (country/city, derived from your IP address, which Google Analytics 4 does not store), device and browser type, referring website, and one event when you run a check, containing only the file type, whether a job ad was added and the score range (for example "80–89"). No content from your CV or the job ad is ever sent.

Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time via Cookie settings; previously set analytics cookies are then deleted.

Retention: Google Analytics keeps event data for 2 months, after which it is deleted automatically.

Transfers outside the EU: Google may process data in the United States. Google LLC is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More information: Google Privacy Policy and how Google Analytics safeguards data.

6. Hosting and technical access logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and libraries are delivered by cdnjs (Cloudflare, Inc.). Like any web server, they receive your IP address and browser information to deliver the page and protect against abuse. This processing is necessary to show you the website (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not receive your CV or any account data, most requests concern Google Analytics data; contact us at the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Malta this is the Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC).

8. Children

This site is not intended for children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Bir CV yüklediğinizde (PDF, DOCX veya TXT), metin ya da iş ilanı yapıştırdığınızda tüm okuma ve analiz kendi cihazınızda, tarayıcınızın içinde yapılır. PDF ve Word dosyalarını okumak için tarayıcınız cdnjs'den iki açık kaynak kütüphane (pdf.js ve mammoth.js) indirir. Bu yalnızca kod indirmesidir; dosyanız cdnjs'e gönderilmez.
