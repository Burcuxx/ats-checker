How an ATS reads your CV
Your CV goes through four stages before a person opens it. It can drop out at any of them.

      

        
1Parsing
The file is turned into plain text. Scans, tables and two-column layouts are where text gets scrambled or lost.

        
2Sorting
The text is split into fields such as experience, education and skills, guided by your section headings.

        
3Searching
Recruiters filter candidates using keywords from the ad. If a skill isn't spelled out, you don't show up.

        
4Reading
Finally a person reads your CV. Clear, measurable results are what make them stop scrolling.

      

    

  


  

    

      
FAQ

      

        
Does it cost anything?
Yes. No sign-up or payment needed.

        
Where does my CV go?
No. Your file is processed entirely in your browser and is never sent anywhere.

        
What exactly is an ATS?
An Applicant Tracking System is software companies use to collect applications, extract information from CVs and search for candidates. Workday, Greenhouse and Lever are common examples.

        
What does the score mean?
Checks such as readability, layout, sections and contact details are scored by weight. Bullets are scored proportionally: if half your bullets have no number, you get half the points for that check. If you add a job ad, keyword match makes up 30% of the score. Scores differ between tools because each uses its own criteria.

        
PDF or Word: which is safer?
Both work in most ATS. What matters is that the PDF contains real text (not a scan) and uses a simple single-column layout.

        
Do you use cookies?
Only if you accept them. With your consent we use Google Analytics to count visits and how often the checker is used. Your CV and the job ad are never sent to Google or anywhere else. You can change your choice any time via Cookie settings at the bottom of the page.
